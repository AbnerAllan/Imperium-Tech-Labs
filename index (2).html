How we work

Brief to live
in weeks, not months

A lean process that keeps you in the loop at every milestone — not just at the end. No surprises, no black boxes.

01
Discovery

We start inside your operations — mapping workflows, identifying bottlenecks, and defining what success looks like for your specific team and context.

02
Architecture

Data models, user roles, integrations, screen flows — all designed before we touch code. You see and approve the full system blueprint first.

03
Build

Iterative sprints with weekly working builds. You test real software at every milestone — not a demo, the actual product.

04
Launch + Support

We deploy, train your team hands-on, and stay on for 30 days post-launch. Everything is documented. Nothing depends on us being available.
